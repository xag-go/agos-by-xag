# Content calendar

1. Start from the workspace's goals (the Vision page) and last week's results.
2. List 5-10 pieces: title, channel, format, owner (@member), due date.
3. Keep one table in the card body; each row becomes its own card for its owner.
4. Leave room for one trend piece the researcher brings in mid-week.
