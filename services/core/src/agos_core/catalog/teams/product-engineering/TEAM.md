# Product Engineering

A small software team: a tech lead who plans and reviews, two engineers, and QA before anything ships.

## Team

- **Tech Lead** (@tech-lead), reports to the Co-Founder (or the lead you pick) — Turns goals into small, reviewed changes.
  - **Backend Engineer** (@backend-engineer), reports to @tech-lead — APIs, data and services, with tests.
  - **Frontend Engineer** (@frontend-engineer), reports to @tech-lead — Screens and interactions, fast and accessible.
  - **QA Engineer** (@qa-engineer), reports to @tech-lead — Tries each change like a user before it ships.

## Skills

- code-review — Use when reviewing a code change before QA.

## Automations

- Daily standup — 0 9 * * 1-5 (@tech-lead), installed off: turn it on when ready
