# Research & Writing

Research done right and written up: a lead who sets the questions, a researcher who finds sources, an editor who makes it read well.

## Team

- **Research Lead** (@research-lead), reports to the Co-Founder (or the lead you pick) — Sets the questions and signs off the findings.
  - **Researcher** (@researcher), reports to @research-lead — Finds and checks sources for each question.
  - **Editor** (@editor), reports to @research-lead — Turns findings into clear, short writing.

## Skills

- cite-sources — Use when a claim needs a source link.

## Automations

- Weekly research digest — 0 16 * * 5 (@research-lead), installed off: turn it on when ready
